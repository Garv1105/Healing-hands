How we treat

## Back to play, in 4 phases

A clear, staged return — you move up only when you pass each step.

-

### Settle

Calm the injury and keep training whatever is safe, so you don’t lose fitness.

Move on when: pain settles with daily movement

-

### Rebuild

Restore strength, mobility and control — and fix what caused the injury.

Move on when: strength matches the other side

-

### Load

Sport-specific drills: running, jumping, cutting, bowling or lifting, built up step by step.

Move on when: you train at full intensity without pain

-

### Return to play

Back to matches and races, with a plan to stay injury-free.

Goal: playing at your best again

Questions

## Before you book

Do I have to stop training completely?

Often not. We'll tell you what you can keep doing safely and what to pause while the injury heals.

When can I play again?

It depends on the injury. You'll get a staged plan after your assessment, so you know what to expect.

Is there a Healing Hands clinic near me?

We have three clinics in South Bangalore — JP Nagar (Sarakki Main Road), Jayanagar 4th Block and BTM Layout 2nd Stage — serving JP Nagar, Jayanagar, BTM, Basavanagudi, Banashankari, HSR Layout, Koramangala, Bannerghatta Road and nearby areas. If you're further away, call us and we'll tell you which clinic is easiest to reach.

How much does physiotherapy cost?

A consultation is ₹500. A specialist or orthopaedician consultation is ₹800. Both include an assessment and the first treatment. Any package is explained after your first session — no surprises.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. You can book directly. If you have scans or reports, bring them along.

Can I see a female physiotherapist?

Yes. Female physiotherapists are available at all three clinics — just mention it when you book.

What are your timings?

Monday to Saturday. JP Nagar and Jayanagar: 8 AM – 1 PM and 5 PM – 9 PM. BTM Layout: 8 AM – 9 PM without a break.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the problem. After your first assessment your physiotherapist tells you what’s going on and how many sessions to expect.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel or reschedule free with at least 2 hours' notice by call or WhatsApp on [888-444-7808](tel:+918884447808) .

How long is a session?

Usually 45 to 60 minutes, depending on your treatment.

Which languages do your physiotherapists speak?

Kannada, Hindi and English at our clinics. Some of our physiotherapists also speak Tamil, Malayalam and other South Indian languages — mention your preference when you book.

I find stairs difficult. Which clinic is easiest?

JP Nagar is on the ground floor with no stairs. BTM Layout is on the first floor with a lift. Jayanagar is a few steps down from the road.

Do you accept insurance?

We don't process insurance claims directly. We give you a detailed bill you can submit to your insurer.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes that let us see and move the area we’re treating — shorts for knee problems, a loose top for shoulder or neck.

Will the treatment hurt?

Your physiotherapist adjusts every technique to your comfort and explains what they're doing before they start. Some techniques can feel uncomfortable for a moment; tell us and we'll change the approach.
