## Treatment by Symptoms

### Back Pain

Determining the cause behind back pain is not enough, as you need to focus on multiple treatments and therapies to produce better results.

[Know More](https://hhphysio.com/symptoms/back-pain/)

### Neck Pain

The neck is made out of vertebrates that start from skull to upper torso. Cervical discs will absorb shocks between bones.

[Know More](https://hhphysio.com/symptoms/neck-pain/)

### Knee Pain

The knee joint is one of the largest joints of the human body. It will help you to walk, run or dance. Damage to any structure in the knee joint can impact normal movement.

[Know More](https://hhphysio.com/symptoms/knee-pain/)

### Muscle Stiffness

Muscle stiffness, also known as stiffness or tightness in the muscles. During muscle stiffness you will find it more difficult to move than you generally do after rest. In short stiff muscle impairs movement and flexibility.

[Know More](https://hhphysio.com/symptoms/muscle-stiffness/)
