### FAQ Page

#### What is a Physiotherapist?

Physiotherapists are university trained and are experts in injury diagnosis, injury treatment, exercise prescription, injury prevention, rehabilitation and many other areas of sport and health and fitness.

#### What do I need to bring?

Be prepared to provide your previous medical or surgical history. If you have them and if they are relevant bring scan/MRI reports, and list of medication. You may wish to bring shorts and t-shirt (or any loose or comfortable dress for stretching)

#### How many visits will I need?

The number of treatments will vary depending on the problem and individual. After a thorough assessment, the physiotherapist will discuss their treatment plan for the rehabilitation process.

#### What should I wear to my appointment?

Wear something comfortable. Bring a pair of shorts and t-shirt if it is a lower back, hip, knee, ankle, or a foot problem. Bring a tank top / vest if it is a neck, upper back, shoulder, elbow, or hand problem. If needed, you can change your clothes at clinic as well.

#### How do I make an appointment?

You can get in touch by phone ( (+91) 888 - 444 -7808 ) or use the form under the contact page. Alternatively use the quick and easy [online booking](https://hhphysio.com/book-appointment/) page, which is the method we prefer. For Video consultancy use this [virtual consultancy link](https://hhphysio.com/virtual-physiotherapy-appointment/) .

#### Do I have to be referred by a doctor?

No, you don't need any other doctor's referral to see a physiotherapist. Our physiotherapists are experts in Neuromusculoskeletal systems. They can do detailed assessments, diagnose your problem and provide proper treatments.

### How long will I need physical therapy?

Treatment duration depends on your diagnosis, the severity of your impairments, your past medical history, and other factors. Re-evaluations will be done to assess your progress and your therapist communicates with your physician to help you achieve your therapy goals.

### What conditions does physical therapy treat?

We sees and treats patient for a wide range of conditions and diagnoses. Please visit our [Services](https://hhphysio.com/services/) for an extensive list of conditions and diagnoses we see patients for.

### Are the physical therapist certified?

Healing Hands Advanced Physiotherapy Clinic hires only those physiotherapists who have completed their 4 and half years of university degree and they have good amount of work experience. Apart from that our physiotherapists are trained compulsorily in many different Advanced therapy courses apart from the university degree. Be assured to have an world class experience with us.

### How do I know if my insurance covers physical therapy services?

We recommend that you confirm your insurance coverage with your insurance provider.

### How do I cancel or reschedule my appointment(s)?

I case you need to cancel or reschedule the appointment, please call us at 8884447808. Please inform atleast 4 hours prior so that we can give that slot to another client .

### How do I book for a Video Consultancy?

Please visit our [Virtual Physiotherapy Appointment](https://hhphysio.com/virtual-physiotherapy-appointment/) page and fill up the required form. Our team will contact you shortly after form submission. You may also call our administration office at +91 888 444 7808 for any queries.
