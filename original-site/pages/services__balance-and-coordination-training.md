What is Balance and Coordination Training?

Balance and coordination training consists of strengthening core muscles to improve the balance & coordination. This training will Improve the communication between your muscles and your brain. Increase core stabilization, which is pertinent for spine care, athletic skills, and. posture.

Conditions Treated:

Our team of highly trained specialist physiotherapists are experts in the treatment of all Balance & Coordination conditions including:

Frequent trips and falls
Poor Balance
Perception difficulties
Poor hand-eye coordination
Gross motor coordination
Restore Mobility
Increase Fitness Level
