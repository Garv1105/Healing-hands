### Symptoms

### Back Pain

Back pain refers to any discomfort or pain experienced in the back region, including the upper, middle, or lower back. It can range from mild, temporary discomfort to severe, debilitating pain that affects daily activities.

[Know More](https://hhphysio.com/symptoms/back-pain/)

### Knee Pain

Knee pain is a common complaint that can affect people of all ages and lifestyles. Whether you're an athlete, a desk worker, or an active senior, understanding knee pain, its causes, symptoms, and treatment options is crucial for maintaining a healthy and active lifestyle.

[Know More](https://hhphysio.com/symptoms/knee-pain/)

### Neck Pain

Neck pain is a very common problem especially in this modern and digital era that affects people of all ages and backgrounds. Whether it's due to poor posture, injury, or underlying medical conditions, neck pain can significantly impact your quality of life.

[Know More](https://hhphysio.com/symptoms/neck-pain/)

### Muscle Stiffness

Muscle stiffness, also known as stiffness or tightness in the muscles. During muscle stiffness you will find it more difficult to move than you generally do after rest. In short stiff muscle impairs movement and flexibility.

[Know More](https://hhphysio.com/symptoms/muscle-stiffness/)
