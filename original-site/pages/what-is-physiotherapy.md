# What is physiotherapy?

In this article you will find out What is Physiotherapy and all the answers related to it.
Physiotherapy utilizes proven techniques to help restore movement and function to anybody affected by injury, disability or health condition. It is a therapy that can help you with accomplishing movement for life.

A physiotherapist will utilize their knowledge on how the body functions, combined with hands-on clinical abilities, to assess, analyze and treat your symptoms. They can even help you prevent many injuries and health conditions.

Physiotherapy is well known for treating injuries, physios also have the skills to treat and prevent a range of health problems including;

- Joints, bones and soft tissue
- Heart
- Chest and lungs
- Brain and nervous system

## Qualified Physios

All physiotherapists are health professional with a valid university degree in physiotherapy. Many proceed to further study and areas of expertise.

## Expertise

Physiotherapy covers a wide range of health issues and physiotherapists frequently decide to focus their mastery on a specific area. Areas of practice can include:

- Acupuncture
- Cardiorespiratory
- Hand Therapy
- Manual Therapy
- Mental Health
- Neurology
- Occupational Health
- Older Adults
- Paediatric
- Pelvic, Women’s and Men’s Health
- Sports and Exercise

You can find a physio with a particular area of practice.

## Why choose physiotherapy?

Physiotherapy can assist you with achieve and maintain movement. Physios are exceptionally qualified health experts who can help with a range of conditions and symptoms.
You can also visit to a physio without any reference on your own; this implies you do not need a doctor’s referral to see a physiotherapist. If you feeling discomfort in movement or having any physical pain then visit a physiotherapist near you.
