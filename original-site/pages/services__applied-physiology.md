## Essence of Applied Physiology

At the heart of Applied Physiology lies the concept of life energy, often known as "Chi" in traditional Chinese medicine. This vital force flows through the body’s energy pathways, or meridians, which connect and influence various organs and systems. When Chi flows freely, the body remains in a state of harmony and vitality. However, blockages in these energy channels can lead to a spectrum of physical, emotional, and spiritual imbalances.

Applied Physiology employs a sophisticated system rooted in kinesiology to detect and rectify these disruptions. The focus is on gentle, non-invasive correction, allowing the body to restore its natural equilibrium and optimal health.
