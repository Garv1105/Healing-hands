# Meet Our Team

Healing Hands Advanced Physiotherapy Clinic is recommended and trusted by many healthcare companies, specialists and hundreds of patients across Bangalore. Our team of Physiotherapists specialize in providing personalized medical care, rehabilitative therapy and companion assistance. Apart from physiotherapy our team also has Consultant Physician. As a result of the hard-work and dedication of a brilliant team we now have 3 clinics across Bangalore... but we're not finished yet! That's why we keep on working hard so that you can get best possible care at affordable price.

Three Physiotherapy Clinics Available! See [physiotherapist near you](https://hhphysio.com/physiotherapist-near-me/)
